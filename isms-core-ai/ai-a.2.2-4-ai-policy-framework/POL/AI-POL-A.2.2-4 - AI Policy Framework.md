## Executive Summary

This policy establishes [Organisation]'s requirements for documenting, aligning, and maintaining a policy framework governing the responsible development and use of AI systems — in accordance with ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Controls A.2.2, A.2.3, and A.2.4.

**Scope**: All AI systems developed or used by [Organisation]; all organisational policies that intersect with AI objectives; the review and maintenance cycle for the AI policy suite.

**Purpose**: Define WHAT the AI policy framework must contain, WHO holds accountability for policy maintenance and alignment, and WHEN reviews and updates are required. Implementation procedures (HOW) are documented in AI-IMP-A.2.2-4-UG and AI-IMP-A.2.2-4-TG.

**Combined Control Rationale**: A.2.2 (AI policy), A.2.3 (alignment with other policies), and A.2.4 (review) form the policy management foundation of the AIMS. The AI policy (A.2.2) establishes the strategic direction; alignment with other organisational policies (A.2.3) ensures there are no gaps or contradictions; and the review cycle (A.2.4) ensures the policy remains current. These three controls are managed as a single framework.

## Scope and Applicability

### ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Control Statements

**Control A.2.2 — AI policy**
The organisation shall document a policy for the development or use of AI systems.

**Control A.2.3 — Alignment with other organisational policies**
The organisation shall determine where other policies can be affected by or apply to the organisation's objectives with respect to AI systems.

**Control A.2.4 — Review of the AI policy**
The AI policy shall be reviewed at planned intervals or additionally as needed to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy and effectiveness.

### What This Policy Covers

- Requirements for the content of the AI policy document
- Responsible AI principles that the AI policy must embody
- Process for identifying and updating policies affected by AI objectives (A.2.3)
- Review schedule and triggers for the AI policy (A.2.4)
- Communication and accessibility requirements for the AI policy

### What This Policy Does NOT Cover

- AI risk assessment methodology (addressed in AI-POL-A.5.2-5 and AIMS Risk Assessment Procedure)
- AI system-specific governance decisions (addressed in AI-POL-01)
- Control-specific implementation requirements (addressed in respective AI-POL-A.x.x policies)

### Regulatory Framework

**Tier 1: Mandatory Compliance** (per AI-POL-00):

- **EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)**: Articles 9 and 17 require quality management system and technical documentation for high-risk AI providers — the AI policy is a foundational element; Article 53 requires GPAI model providers to implement policies for copyright compliance

**Tier 2: Conditional** (per AI-POL-00):

- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023**: Controls A.2.2, A.2.3, A.2.4 — applies where AIMS certification is in scope or contractually required

**Tier 3: Informational** (per AI-POL-00):

- NIST AI RMF: GOVERN function — organisational AI policies and culture
- OECD AI Principles: Accountability and responsible AI governance
- ISO/IEC 38507:2022: Governing body AI policy responsibilities

## Policy Statements: AI Policy Content (A.2.2)

### AI Policy Document Requirement

[Organisation] SHALL maintain a documented AI Policy that is approved by top management, communicated within the organisation, and available to relevant interested parties.

### Mandatory Content of the AI Policy

The AI Policy SHALL include:

**1. Statement of AI principles and commitments:**

[Organisation] is committed to the responsible development and use of AI systems. The AI Policy shall articulate specific responsible AI principles including:

- **Fairness**: AI systems should treat individuals and groups equitably; bias shall be identified and mitigated
- **Transparency**: The use of AI and the basis for AI-driven decisions shall be disclosed appropriately to affected parties
- **Accountability**: Clear human accountability for AI system behaviour and outcomes shall be maintained at all times
- **Safety**: AI systems shall not pose unacceptable risks to individuals, society, or the organisation
- **Privacy**: AI systems shall process personal data in accordance with applicable data protection obligations (co-reference PRIV-POL-00)
- **Reliability**: AI systems shall perform as documented within defined operating conditions
- **Human oversight**: AI systems making consequential decisions shall be subject to meaningful human oversight proportionate to impact level

**2. Scope of the AI policy:**

The AI Policy shall define which AI systems, organisational functions, and activities it governs. The scope shall be consistent with the AIMS Scope Document (AI-POL-01).

**3. Commitment to comply with applicable requirements:**

The AI Policy shall include an explicit commitment to comply with applicable AI laws and standards as determined in AI-POL-00. This includes EU AI Act obligations for the organisation's AI systems and ISO 42001:2023 where certification is sought.

**4. Commitment to AI risk and impact assessment:**

The AI Policy shall include a commitment to conduct AI risk assessments (per Clause 6.1.2) and AI System Impact Assessments (AISIA, per Clause 6.1.4 and ISO 42005:2025) for all in-scope AI systems before deployment and at defined review intervals.

**5. Commitment to continual improvement:**

The AI Policy shall include a commitment to the continual improvement of the AIMS.

**6. Commitment to human oversight:**

The AI Policy shall specify that AI systems are subject to human oversight proportionate to their potential impact on individuals and society.

**7. Processes for handling deviations and exceptions:**

The AI Policy shall include reference to the process for handling exceptions to responsible AI principles, including escalation path and documentation requirements.

### AI Policy Format and Accessibility

- The AI Policy shall be a single authoritative document, not a collection of implicit commitments across multiple documents
- It shall be available to all employees, contractors, and relevant third parties
- It shall be published in the languages relevant to the organisation's operational jurisdictions
- It shall bear the signature or formal endorsement of top management (CEO or equivalent)

## Policy Statements: Alignment with Other Policies (A.2.3)

### Policy Alignment Requirement

[Organisation] SHALL systematically identify and document all organisational policies that are affected by or apply to the organisation's AI objectives, and ensure they are aligned with the AI Policy.

### Policies Requiring AI Alignment Assessment

The AI Governance Officer shall assess the following policy domains for AI-related content gaps or conflicts:

| Policy Domain | AI Intersection | Action Required |
|--------------|----------------|----------------|
| **Information Security (ISMS)** | AI system security requirements, AI-driven threat detection, AI in ISMS operations | Cross-reference AI security controls (A.6.2.4, A.6.2.6) with ISMS-POL-A.8.x |
| **Data Protection / Privacy (PIMS)** | AI processing personal data, automated decision-making (GDPR Art. 22), AI training data with PII | Align with PRIV-POL-00; co-reference PRIV-POL-A.1.3.11 |
| **Human Resources** | AI use in hiring/performance assessment, AI-assisted decisions affecting employees, AI competence requirements | Update HR policies to reflect AI acceptable use and oversight obligations |
| **Procurement / Supply Chain** | Procurement of AI tools and services, AI supplier due diligence | Align with AI-POL-A.10.2-4 |
| **Code of Conduct / Ethics** | Responsible AI use by employees, reporting AI-related concerns | Update to include responsible AI use obligations and concerns reporting (A.3.3) |
| **IT Acceptable Use** | Employee use of public AI tools (e.g., generative AI services), data classification rules for AI input | Update to reflect AI-specific acceptable use rules and data handling |
| **Quality Management** | Quality criteria for AI-generated outputs, AI in quality control processes | Align with A.6.2.4 (verification and validation) |
| **Change Management** | AI system changes, model updates, material enhancements triggering re-assessment | Ensure AI system changes trigger AISIA review |

### Alignment Documentation

For each policy domain assessed, the AI Governance Officer shall document:

- Whether the policy is affected by AI objectives
- What updates are required (if any)
- Who is responsible for implementing the update
- Target completion date
- Review outcome

The policy alignment assessment shall be documented in the AI Policy Alignment Register, maintained by the AI Governance Officer.

### Gap Handling

Where a policy gap is identified that cannot be addressed through an update to an existing policy, the AI Governance Officer may:

- Create a new AI-specific policy document (e.g., an AI Acceptable Use Policy as a standalone supplement)
- Issue an interim directive pending policy update
- Document the gap as an open improvement item in the management review agenda

Gaps shall not be left undocumented or unaddressed.

## Policy Statements: Review of the AI Policy (A.2.4)

### Review Schedule

The AI Policy shall be reviewed at a minimum **annually**, coordinated with the annual AIMS management review (AI-POL-01, Clause 9.3).

### Review Triggers

The following events trigger an out-of-cycle review of the AI Policy:

- New AI regulation enacted or significantly updated in a jurisdiction where the organisation operates
- Significant expansion or change in the organisation's AI portfolio (new high-risk AI system, new AI use cases)
- Material AI incident that reveals a gap in the current policy commitments
- Internal audit finding that identifies policy inadequacy
- Certification body feedback on policy scope or content
- Management review decision requiring policy update

### Review Process

1. AI Governance Officer prepares a review summary including: regulatory changes, AI portfolio changes, audit findings, and any AIMS performance issues since last review
2. Review is conducted by: AI Governance Officer, CISO, Legal/Compliance, and relevant AI System Owners
3. Changes are documented with rationale
4. Updated policy is approved by top management
5. Updated policy is communicated and re-distributed

### Review Outcomes

Possible outcomes of an AI Policy review:

| Outcome | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Policy remains suitable | Document review date and no-change determination; retain as evidence |
| Minor update required | Update policy; re-approve; communicate changes |
| Significant update required | Full policy redraft; top management re-endorsement; reissue with new version number |
| Scope change required | Trigger AIMS scope review per AI-POL-01 |

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|----------------|
| **AI Governance Officer** | Authors and maintains AI Policy; conducts policy alignment assessment (A.2.3); chairs annual review (A.2.4); ensures top management endorsement |
| **Executive Management (CEO)** | Endorses and formally approves AI Policy; communicates commitment to responsible AI |
| **CISO** | Reviews AI Policy for alignment with ISMS; advises on security-related responsible AI commitments |
| **Legal / Compliance** | Reviews AI Policy for compliance with AI-POL-00 Tier 1 obligations (EU AI Act, GDPR Art. 22); advises on regulatory change triggers |
| **All Employees** | Comply with AI Policy; report deviations or concerns per A.3.3 process |

## Audit Considerations

Auditors verifying compliance with A.2.2–A.2.4 should expect to find:

- A single, approved AI Policy document endorsed by top management
- Policy content covering all mandatory elements (principles, scope, compliance commitment, risk/impact assessment commitment, human oversight, continual improvement)
- Evidence of policy alignment assessment across relevant organisational policies
- Annual review records with documented outcomes
- Communication evidence that the AI Policy is accessible to relevant parties
